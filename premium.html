Frequently Asked Questions About the Premium Plan

Why choose Premium over Standard Plan?
The Premium Plan includes 5-7 pages instead of 3-5, premium custom design with advanced UI/UX, technical SEO with schema markup, Core Web Vitals optimization, performance optimization, blog-ready structure, portfolio section, and 60 days of premium support. It's the complete solution for businesses ready to dominate online.
What makes Premium different from Standard?
Premium offers more pages (5-7 vs 3-5), premium custom design, advanced technical SEO, schema markup, Core Web Vitals optimization, blog-ready structure, portfolio section, and 60 days of support vs 30 days. It's a complete premium solution.
How many pages are included in the Premium Plan?
The Premium Plan includes 5 to 7 professional pages — typically Home, About, Services, Solutions/Products, Portfolio, Blog/Resources, and Contact. You can customize the pages based on your business needs.
Is blog support included in the Premium Plan?
Yes! The Premium Plan includes a blog-ready structure so you can start publishing content to improve your SEO and engage customers. We set up the blog foundation for you.
Can e-commerce be added later to the Premium Plan?
Yes! The Premium Plan is built with scalability in mind. We can add e-commerce functionality at any time. Contact us for a custom quote.
Is technical SEO and schema markup included?
Yes! The Premium Plan includes advanced technical SEO with schema markup (Article, Product, LocalBusiness, FAQ schemas), Core Web Vitals optimization, and comprehensive on-page SEO across all pages.
Will I own my domain and website?
Yes! The domain is registered in YOUR business name. You get full ownership of the domain and all website files. We just help you set everything up professionally.
How long does delivery take for the Premium Plan?
The Premium Plan is delivered within 14-18 working days from the day you share your content and pay the 50% advance. This allows time for premium design, thorough testing, and optimization.
What support is included with the Premium Plan?
The Premium Plan includes 60 days of premium support after launch — for any bugs, tweaks, content updates, or questions. Priority WhatsApp support is included.
What are the renewal charges after the first year?
From the 2nd year, only domain renewal is needed – approximately ₹800–₹1,200/year, paid directly to the domain registrar. We do not mark up this cost.
How is payment made for the Premium Plan?
Payment is 50% advance and 50% before final handover. We accept UPI, bank transfer (NEFT/IMPS), and standard payment apps.
Can I upgrade or customize features later?
Yes! The Premium Plan is fully customizable. You can add e-commerce, membership features, booking systems, or any other functionality as your business grows.
