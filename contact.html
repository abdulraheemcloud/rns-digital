FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly do you respond to enquiries?
We typically respond within 2-4 hours on working days (Mon–Sat, 9 AM – 7 PM IST). WhatsApp responses are usually faster.
How long does website development take?
Basic plan: 7 working days. Standard plan: 10-14 working days. Premium plan: 14-18 working days. Timeline starts once content and advance payment are received.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept UPI, bank transfer (NEFT/IMPS), and standard payment apps. Payment terms: 50% advance and 50% before final handover.
Who owns the website after completion?
You do! The domain is registered in your business name and you get full ownership of the website. We just help you set everything up.
Do you provide support after launch?
Yes! All our plans include free support after launch (7, 30, or 60 days depending on the plan). We're always available via WhatsApp for any questions or issues.
Can I request revisions during development?
Yes! We provide 2 rounds of revisions per plan. We share design previews for your feedback and make adjustments until you're satisfied.
Do you provide hosting services?
Yes, hosting is included in the first year of all our plans. From the second year, you can continue with us or move to any hosting provider of your choice.
Is there any ongoing maintenance cost?
No. Maintenance is completely optional. Your website runs independently without any mandatory monthly fees. We offer optional maintenance plans if you need regular updates.
