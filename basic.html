FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About the Basic Plan

What is included in the Basic Plan?
The Basic Plan includes a 1-page professional website, mobile responsive design, WhatsApp chat, click-to-call, contact form, Google Maps integration, basic SEO, Google Business setup, 1-year domain, free hosting, SSL security, 7-day delivery, and 7 days support.
Who is the Basic Plan best for?
The Basic Plan is ideal for small businesses, startups, shops, local businesses, professionals, and freelancers who need a professional online presence quickly and affordably.
Can I upgrade from Basic to Standard later?
Yes! You can upgrade anytime by paying the difference. We'll add all features from the Standard plan to your existing website.
Do I own my domain with the Basic Plan?
Yes! The domain is registered in YOUR business name. You get full ownership. We just help you set it up.
How long does delivery take for the Basic Plan?
The Basic Plan is delivered within 7 working days from the day you share your content and pay the 50% advance.
Will my Basic Plan website rank on Google?
Yes! The Basic Plan includes basic SEO setup with proper title tags, meta descriptions, heading structure, image alt tags, and sitemap. We also set up your Google Business Profile to help with local search visibility.

How is payment made for the Basic Plan?
Payment is 50% advance and 50% before final handover. We accept UPI, bank transfer (NEFT/IMPS), and standard payment apps.
