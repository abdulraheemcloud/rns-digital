FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About the Standard Plan

Why choose Standard over Basic Plan?
The Standard Plan includes 3-5 pages instead of 1, advanced on-page SEO, local keyword optimization, Google Business Profile optimization, lead tracking, analytics setup, image gallery, testimonials, and 30 days of support — everything you need to generate more leads and rank higher on Google.
How many pages are included in the Standard Plan?
The Standard Plan includes 3 to 5 professional pages — typically Home, About, Services, Gallery/Portfolio, Testimonials, and Contact. You can choose the pages that best suit your business.
Is SEO included in the Standard Plan?
Yes! The Standard Plan includes advanced on-page SEO with keyword optimization, proper heading structure, meta tags, image optimization, sitemap, and Google Search Console setup. We also optimize your Google Business Profile for better local visibility.
Do you optimize Google Business Profile?
Yes! The Standard Plan includes complete Google Business Profile optimization with photos, categories, description, service areas, and regular updates to help you appear in local search results.
Can I upgrade from Standard to Premium later?
Yes! You can upgrade anytime by paying the difference. We'll add all features from the Premium plan to your existing website.
Do I own my domain and website?
Yes! The domain is registered in YOUR business name. You get full ownership of the domain and website files. We just help you set everything up.
How long does delivery take for the Standard Plan?
The Standard Plan is delivered within 10-14 working days from the day you share your content and pay the 50% advance.
What support is included with the Standard Plan?
The Standard Plan includes 30 days of free support after launch — for any bugs, tweaks, content updates, or questions. We're available via WhatsApp.
What are the renewal charges after the first year?
From the 2nd year, only domain renewal is needed – approximately ₹800–₹1,200/year, paid directly to the domain registrar. We do not mark up this cost.
How is payment made for the Standard Plan?
Payment is 50% advance and 50% before final handover. We accept UPI, bank transfer (NEFT/IMPS), and standard payment apps.
