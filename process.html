Our Process

Our 7-Step Development Process

Every project follows the same reliable process. We keep you informed at every stage.

01

Free Consultation

We start with a simple WhatsApp or phone conversation to understand your business, goals, and budget. We'll recommend the right plan and give you a clear written quote with no hidden charges.

02

Requirement Collection

We collect everything we need to build your website: logo, business details, services, photos, domain preference, and social media links. We guide you through the process.

03

Planning & Design

We create wireframes, layout designs, color schemes, and mobile-first planning. You receive a design preview within 4-5 days for your feedback.

04

Website Development

We build your website with responsive coding, SEO optimization, fast loading, and clean code. Every site follows our quality standards.

05

Client Review

We share a live preview link for your review. You provide feedback, we make revisions, and you approve the final version before launch.

06

Testing & Launch

We test on mobile, tablet, and desktop. We check SEO, performance, forms, and security. After final approval, we deploy your website live.

07

Support

We provide training on how to update basic content. Free support included for 7, 30, or 60 days depending on your plan. We're always available via WhatsApp.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the website development process take?
Basic plan: 7 working days. Standard plan: 10-14 working days. Premium plan: 14-18 working days. Timeline starts once content and advance payment are received.
How many revisions can I request?
We provide 2 rounds of revisions per plan. Additional revisions can be requested at a nominal fee. We work closely with you to ensure the final result matches your vision.
Who owns the website after completion?
You do! The domain is registered in your business name and you get full ownership of the website. We just help you set everything up.
Do you provide hosting services?
Yes, hosting is included in the first year of all our plans. From the second year, you can continue with us or move to any hosting provider of your choice.
Can I edit the website myself after launch?
Yes! We provide training and guidance on how to update basic content. You can also contact us for any updates at a nominal maintenance fee.
What information do I need to provide?
We need your business name, logo, photos, service details, contact information, domain preference, and social media links. We'll guide you through everything.
Do you test websites before launch?
Yes! Every website undergoes thorough testing on mobile, tablet, and desktop. We also check SEO, performance, forms, and security before launch.
What support do you provide after launch?
All our plans include free support after launch (7, 30, or 60 days depending on the plan). We're always available via WhatsApp for any questions or issues.
