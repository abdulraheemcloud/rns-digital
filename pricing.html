FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the price fixed or will there be extra charges?
The plan price is fixed. No extra charges from us. Domain and hosting from 2nd year are paid by you directly to the provider (approx ₹1,000/year).
Can I upgrade from Basic to Standard or Premium later?
Yes. You can upgrade any time by paying the difference. We'll add all features from your new plan to your existing website.
What payment methods do you accept?
UPI, bank transfer (NEFT/IMPS), and standard payment apps. We take 50% advance and 50% before final handover.
How long does delivery take?
Basic plan: 7 working days. Standard: 10–14 days. Premium: 14–18 days. Timeline starts once you share your content and 50% advance is received.
Do I own the domain and website?
Yes! Domain is registered in YOUR business name. You get full ownership. We just help you set it up.
Is monthly support compulsory?
No. Monthly support is completely optional. Your website runs independently without it.
