FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What makes RNS Digital different from other web development companies?
RNS Digital focuses on transparent pricing, fast delivery, SEO-optimized websites, and real results. We never use hidden charges, and every client gets full ownership of their domain and website.
Do you offer transparent pricing?
Yes! All our prices are publicly listed on our pricing page. There are no hidden charges. The only ongoing cost is domain renewal approximately ₹1,000/year paid directly to the registrar.
How fast can you deliver a website?
Our Basic plan is delivered in 7 working days. Standard and Premium plans take 10-18 working days depending on the complexity. We always provide a clear timeline upfront.
Will my website be SEO optimized?
Yes! All our websites include SEO optimization — from basic setup to advanced technical SEO and schema markup depending on your chosen plan.
Do I own my domain and website?
Yes! Your domain is registered in your business name and you get full ownership of the website. We just help you set everything up.
What support do you provide after launch?
All our plans include free support after launch (7, 30, or 60 days depending on the plan). We're always available via WhatsApp for any questions or issues.
Is my website mobile responsive?
Absolutely! Every website we build is fully responsive and works perfectly on phones, tablets, and desktops.
How do I get started with RNS Digital?
Simply contact us through the contact form, WhatsApp, or phone. We'll schedule a free consultation to understand your needs and provide a custom quote.
